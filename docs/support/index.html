Quick troubleshooting

  • Camera permission: Ensure the app has Camera access in system Settings.
  • Dark screen: Increase ambient light or enable the torch if available.
  • No effect from sliders: Point at moving wheels/zoetrope patterns; adjust Hz to sync, and Exposure (0.1–0.9 ms) to freeze/smear motion.
  • Performance varies: Behavior can differ by device camera and lighting conditions.

FAQ

What do the sliders do?
Hz sets a rotational sync (RPM). Exposure controls shutter time for motion effects.

Does the app collect data?
No. Processing is on‑device. See the Privacy Policy.

Flashing images?
Parts of the app may display flashing imagery.
