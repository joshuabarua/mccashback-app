Automation in D Minor

Camera‑powered generative art. Adjust Hz and exposure to reveal motion.

About

Automation is a camera‑driven, generative art instrument. Point your phone at moving wheels or zoetrope patterns and tune two sliders to sync motion and reveal hidden animations in real time.

Features

  • Hz/RPM slider with format‑aware stops
  • Manual exposure slider (0.1–0.9 ms)
  • Optional torch and drift mode
  • On‑screen readouts for Hz and RPM
  • On‑device processing; no accounts, no analytics

Safety

Flashing images warning: parts of the app may display flashing imagery.
